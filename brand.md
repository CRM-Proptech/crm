# Brand — Keystone

_Status: active_

Property-native CRM. Warm architectural workstation, not generic SaaS blue.

## Palette

| Token | Light | Dark | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paper | `oklch(0.96 0.012 85)` | `oklch(0.20 0.015 50)` | App background |
| Ink | `oklch(0.22 0.02 55)` | `oklch(0.93 0.01 85)` | Body text |
| Brass | `oklch(0.48 0.12 45)` | `oklch(0.68 0.11 50)` | Primary actions, scores |
| Stone | `oklch(0.42 0.02 55)` | `oklch(0.72 0.015 70)` | Secondary text |

## Typography

- **Display / names:** Newsreader
- **UI:** Geist Sans
- **Numbers:** Geist Mono, `tabular-nums`

## Voice

Short, specific, sales-floor language. "Uncontacted since Tuesday." not "No recent engagement activity."

## Direction

Workstation-dense, warm monochrome, one brass accent. Property is a first-class object.
